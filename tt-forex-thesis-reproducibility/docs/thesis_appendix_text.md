## Appendix X. Research code and reproducibility archive

The archived repository, *Multi-Currency TT Forecasting for Sri Lanka*, records the source notebooks, selected tabular outputs and provenance documents supporting the thesis. The experiments cover data preparation, forecasting benchmarks, statistical and machine-learning methods, adaptive and online hybrid approaches, recurrent models, corrected volatility evaluation, sensitivity analysis and the locked 2024–2025 point-forecast evaluation.

Code and outputs are organized by experimental stage. The final 2024–2025 locked holdout is distinct from the 2019–2023 development comparisons, and the corrected volatility validation is distinguished from earlier exploratory volatility experiments.
